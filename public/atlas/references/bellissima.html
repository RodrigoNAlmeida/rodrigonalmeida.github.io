Bellissima’s construction

Explore this construction →
Motivation

Bellissima’s universal model gives a representation of the free Heyting algebra on two generators by building the "image-finite part" of the dual space. The construction is recursive, and proceeds by layers, taking into account the structure of successors and the "colors" -- the propositional variables satisfied by each point.

Bellissima’s universal model U(2) is the image-finite part part of X2. We begin with four maximal points, one of each colour ∅, {p}, {q}, {p,q}.

At each round we build a new layer of U(2) as follows: pick a finite antichain A from the existing model, containing at least one point from the preceding layer, and a color C which is below the color of each point in this antichain; then add a point which has A as its successors, and is colored C.

The interactive display shows the four maximal points, and a selection of 18 points (the layers grow extremely quickly -- at the third layer several hundreds or thousands of points). The color upsets in the end are given by the points sharing the colors p and q.

At the end of the construction, the poset will contain a copy of each (irreducible) 2-generated Kripke model.

Details of the construction

Choose a nonempty finite antichain A in the existing model, containing a point from the preceding layer. For each colour C contained in every colour on A, add the unique point x whose immediate successors are exactly A. A singleton support requires a strict colour drop.

col(x) = C ⊆ ⋂a∈A col(a)
↑x ∖ {x} = ↑A
A = {a} ⇒ C ⊊ col(a)

This partial view has 18 points across four layers: all four maxima, followed by 4, 5, and 5 selected points. Every ancestor of every selected point is included, so all displayed Hasse lines are genuine covers in U(2), including lines that skip a row.

The two colour upsets are p = {x : p ∈ col(x)} and q = {x : q ∈ col(x)}.
