Mardaev frames

Explore this construction →
Motivation

The dual algebras were introduced to study pre-locally tabular superintuitionistic logics and to prove that there are continuum many of them.

Details of the construction

Begin with a maximal point and a three point antichain below it. We give the maximum the colour {p,q}, and {p}, {q}, and ∅ for each point on the antichain.

A Mardaev letter Mn+2 is a sequence of a three point antichain, followed by n many points. The comparisons are made as follows:

xk+1 < xk,   yk+1 < yk
xk+1 < yk−1,   yk+1 < xk−1

When stacking together different Mardaev letters, we can make a Mardaev word Mw. This is done by gluing together two Mardaev letters along their antichains.

Given an ω-word w∈Σω, for Σ a finite alphabet, this allows us to construct one Mardaev frame for each ω-word. Special Mardaev frames -- those corresponding to the algebras studied by Mardaev in his 1987 paper -- give rise to pre-locally tabular logics.
