WB graph frame

Explore this construction →
Motivation

This frame was introduced in our studies of pre-local tabularity and local tabularity, seeking a width-bounded locally tabular logic: it is not locally tabular, but all finitely generated algebras of bounded width in its variety are finite. Every finitely generated algebra in this variety is co-Hopfian.

Details of the construction

This frame is constructed layer by layer, employing specific graphs -- so called θ-graphs -- to determine the attachment of points. Specifically, one starts with an almost complete graph G0 with four vertices and 5 edges, and obtains the layer D0.

The elements of the graph G1 consist of the edges of G0. This gives the layer D1, where an edge e∈D1 is below all points which it is not connected to. The graph G1 is a pentagon, with one extra connection between v0 and v2, and we repeat the design of the layer D2. More generally:

Di+1 = E(Gi)
e < v ⇔ v ∉ e,   v ∈ Di
|Di+1| = |Di| + 1

The idea of the construction is to ensure that the width grows unboundedly, whilst the poset remains an ω-upset - every point sees finitely many points, and the downset of each point is cofinite. The construction then adds the compactification point ∞. The conditions for how this is achieved are technical, and depend on some graph-theoretic facts.

How do later graphs gain width?

These publications provide related graph transformations, crown posets, graph-packing methods and Esakia constructions; they do not identify the complete WB progression.
