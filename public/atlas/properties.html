IdentifierNameDescription
P000000Priestley spaceCompact Hausdorff space where for each pair of points x,y where x is not below y, there is a clopen upset U where x is in U and y is not in U.
P000001Esakia spaceCompact Hausdorff Priestley space in which the downset of every clopen set is clopen.
P000002At most countableThe underlying set is finite or countably infinite. This concerns the whole construction, including its limit points, rather than the finite diagram window.
P000003FiniteThe underlying set has finitely many points. Finite windows used to draw an infinite construction do not make this property true.
P000004RootedThe order has a least element: a point below every point of the space or frame.
P000005NoetherianThe order has no infinite strictly ascending chain. A nonisolated least point may have transfinite depth without violating this condition.
P000006Finite widthThere is a finite uniform bound on the cardinalities of antichains in the order. For a space or poset, it is assumed only locally: for each rooted upset.
P000007Greatest elementThe order has a greatest element: a point above every point of the space or frame.
P000008Isolated rootThe order has a least element and that point is isolated in the topology.
P000009DiscreteThe topology is discrete (for posets with no specified topology, this is assumed).
P000010bi-Esakia spaceBoth the space and its order-dual are Esakia spaces.
P000011Finite depthThere is a uniform bound on the cardinality of the chains in the order.
P000012Clopen maximumThe maximum of the space is a clopen set.
P000013KM spaceFor every clopen subset, the maximum of the clopen is again clopen.
P000014Extremally order-disconnectedThe upset of the closure of a union of clopen upsets is again a clopen upset.
P000015Extremally disconnectedClosure of a union of opens is open.
P000016Finite incomparability degreeFor every point in the order, there are finitely many points which are incomparable to it.
